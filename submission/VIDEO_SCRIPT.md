# Video script

Target 3:50. Ceiling 5:00. Four speakers on camera. Lines are written for Karina, Hannes, Maalon and Alysa so each pillar has a face. Simone can take any engineering line or run the demo hands in part 3.

Three parts:
1. Cold open, 0:00 to 0:20. No faces. Big words, big numbers, hard cuts.
2. Relay, 0:20 to 1:50. One person, one line, one location. Cut on every line. Nobody speaks for more than five seconds.
3. Split screen, 1:50 to 3:35. Left: today. Right: with us. Same incident, two outcomes, narrated.
4. Close, 3:35 to 3:50.

Rules: every number on screen has a source line in the corner. Numbers marked [target] or [drop test] are not measured yet and must be filled or cut before export. Nothing on screen or spoken may use an em dash.
