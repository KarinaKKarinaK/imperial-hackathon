# Pitch video plan

Target length: 4:00. Hard ceiling 5:00. Script 19 Oct, shoot 22 Oct, cut 23 Oct.

Audience: a hepatologist, a computing lecturer, and a Google strategist. Each needs one moment that is clearly for them. Structure follows Imperial's five steps so the judges can tick boxes while watching.

One rule: every claim on screen has a source in the corner. Every number is one we can defend.

## Beat sheet

## Why this order
- Problem before solution, as Imperial scores problem identification separately.
- Demo at the midpoint, when attention is highest, and before the harder business and ethics content.
- Validation straight after the demo so the demo is not read as a stunt.
- Imperial's four feedback points each get a beat: STEMB (4:00), validation (2:30), insurance (3:00), consent (3:30).

## Production

Shoot
- Phone camera, landscape, 1080p. Tripod or a stack of books.
- Two locations: a street with riders (hook, 5 minutes of footage is enough) and a desk at HKUST for the demo.
- Demo: record screen captures of the app, dispatcher page and underwriter page at the same time as the camera. Sync on the drop sound.
- Each speaker records their lines twice. Pick the better take. Lapel mic if anyone has one, otherwise phone close to the mouth, quiet room.
- Film the drop tests on 20 and 21 Oct anyway. That footage goes in the validation beat.

Edit
- CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, whoever has it.
- Lower thirds: name and pillar on first appearance.
- Source line bottom right on every stat slide.
- No background music under speech. Short sting on the hook only.
- Export 1080p MP4, check size limit on the portal first.

## Script rules
- Short sentences. Say it the way you would to a friend.
- No "revolutionary", "seamless", "leverage", "empower".
- Say what we did not prove at least once.
- Total word count under 560 (about 140 words per minute).

## Fallbacks
- Helmet BLE not stable: do the demo from phone sensors only, show the helmet in the drop test footage.
- Live pipeline too slow on the day: pre-record the demo with visible timestamps and say it is a recording.
- Someone unavailable to film: another member reads their lines, name still shown on their pillar.
