# Rider interviews

Why: the judges said start with the human story and design for real people. Two riders in their own words do more than any chart. Target five short conversations, two on camera, by Wednesday.

## Where and when

- Pick-up clusters outside large malls and McDonald's between 14:00 and 16:30, the lull between lunch and dinner. Mong Kok (Langham Place side streets), Sham Shui Po (Fuk Wa Street), Kwun Tong (apm).
- Two people go. One talks, one holds the phone. Bring the printed consent sheet, a pen, and a phone with metricsfile.vercel.app/rider open.
- Offer a drink. Keep each conversation under eight minutes. Riders lose money while they talk to you.

## Opening line

English: "Hi, we are students at HKUST. We are building a sensor that proves when a rider crashes on a job, so insurance pays out without a fight. Could we ask you three questions? Five minutes. You can stay anonymous."

廣東話: 「你好，我哋係科大嘅學生。我哋整緊一個感應器，可以證明外賣員係送單途中出意外，咁保險就可以直接賠，唔使爭拗。可唔可以問你三條問題？五分鐘，可以唔留名。」

## The three questions

1. Have you, or someone you ride with, crashed while on a delivery? What happened after, with the platform and with money?
   你或者同你一齊做嘅人，有冇試過送單途中出意外？之後平台同錢方面點處理？
2. If a clip on your helmet and your phone could prove the crash happened on a job, and pay out in a day, would you wear it? What would worry you about it?
   如果頭盔上有個夾同埋你部電話可以證明意外係送單時發生，一日內賠錢，你會唔會戴？你會擔心啲乜？
3. Who should be allowed to see that data: you, the platform, the insurer, the government?
   邊個應該可以睇到呢啲數據：你自己、平台、保險公司、定係政府？

Then, if they are willing: hand them the phone on /rider, let them tap through consent and "On shift", and film their hands and the screen. Thirty seconds.

## What to do with it

- Same evening: type up each answer in `research/RIDER_VOICES.md` with rider number, district, platform if offered, years riding, and the three answers.
- Pick two quotes for the video cold open and one for the poster. Keep them verbatim, translate underneath.
- Count: how many had a crash story, how many would wear it, what worried them. Those three numbers go on the site in the Riders tile.
- Anything a rider says that contradicts our design (for example, they want the platform to see the data) goes in the limits section, not in the bin.

## Do not

- Do not ask for names, phone numbers or account screenshots.
- Do not film platform logos on bags if the rider has not agreed to be identified.
- Do not promise the product exists. It is a prototype and a proposal.
