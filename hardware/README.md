# Hardware design

Two design images for the helmet clip, both consistent with the parts list in `firmware/README.md` and the bill of materials on the site.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `exploded-view.png` | Exploded isometric view: PLA top cover with LED window and slide switch, ESP32 DevKit V1, GY-521 (MPU-6050), 500 mAh LiPo, TP4056 USB-C charger, PLA base shell with strap slots and foam pad, elastic strap |
| `enclosure-blueprint.png` | 2D drawing of the enclosure: 60 x 35 x 22 mm, 1.6 mm walls, 20 x 4 mm strap slots, 5 mm LED hole, 9 x 3.5 mm USB-C cutout, four M2 bosses at 46 x 21 mm centres, print orientation lid and base flat |
| `superseded-coin-sized-blueprint.png` | Earlier concept with a custom PCB and ESP32-C3 in a 34 mm puck. Not what we are building. Kept for the record |

Compressed copies of the first two are served from `web/public/hardware/` on the landing page. The CAD files for printing go in this folder when Hannes commits them.
